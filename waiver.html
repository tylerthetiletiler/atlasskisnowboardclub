Please complete the club waiver before participating in club activities.

Before you participate

Download and complete the current employee waiver and release form. Once signed, email it to atlasskisnowboardclub@gmail.com.

The waiver is currently hosted on the club’s existing website so this GitHub Pages migration always points members to the same published form.

Open current waiver PDF ↗

What the waiver covers

The current form addresses the inherent risks of ski-club participation, participant responsibility, release and indemnity provisions, insurance and workers’ compensation limitations, and use of participant name or likeness. Members should read the actual PDF in full before signing.
