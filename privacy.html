Privacy Policy.

How Atlas Ski & Snowboard Club handles information submitted through the website and club activities.

Policy date on the existing site: November 6, 2025.

Migration note: This page restates the substance of the current club policy in a cleaner web format. Before replacing the existing site, the club should compare this version with its approved legal text.

1. Information the club may collect

Information provided directly may include a member’s name, email, phone number, emergency-contact details, membership or registration information, event sign-up details, and other information voluntarily submitted through forms or email.

The website may also receive technical information such as IP address, browser and device data, and general site-usage information for performance, analytics, and security.

2. How information may be used

  • Administer memberships and event registrations.
  • Send important club, event, and safety communications.
  • Maintain emergency-contact and participation records.
  • Improve website functionality and club services.
  • Meet legal or organizational requirements.

The existing policy states that the club does not sell personal data.

3. Storage and security

The club takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. The existing policy notes that certain membership, registration, and administrative records may be stored on Google Drive and therefore processed using Google’s cloud infrastructure.

4. Sharing

Information may be shared with club staff or volunteers when needed to operate events or address safety needs, with service providers used for club administration, or when disclosure is legally required or needed to protect member safety. The existing policy states that information is not shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

5. Cookies and usage technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, understand site usage, and improve the experience. Browser settings can be used to limit cookies, though doing so may affect some features.

6. Choices and requests

Depending on applicable law, individuals may be able to request access, correction, deletion, or changes to certain communications. Requests can be sent to the club email below.

7. Children’s privacy

The existing policy states that the website and club activities are not directed to children under 13 and that the club does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

8. External links

Links to third-party websites may appear on the site. Those sites operate under their own privacy practices.

9. Policy changes

The club may update its privacy practices from time to time and should publish the revised date when it does so.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy or data handling can be sent to atlasskisnowboardclub@gmail.com.
