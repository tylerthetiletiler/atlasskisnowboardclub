About the club

More than a lift ticket.

Atlas Ski & Snowboard Club connects airline-industry skiers and riders through organized mountain weeks, NAASF events, racing, lessons, and social time.

  • Trips for social skiers, snowboarders, and racers
  • Destinations across the U.S. and Europe
  • Member resources, waivers, and event information in one place
