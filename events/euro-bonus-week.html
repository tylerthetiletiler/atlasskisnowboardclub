NAASF Euro Bonus Week

Mar 14–19, 2026 · St. Anton am Arlberg, Austria

● Registration closed

The Euro Bonus Week focused on St. Anton and the broader Ski Arlberg region, known for extensive marked runs, off-piste terrain, modern lifts, and dependable high-alpine conditions. The published trip also called out Ischgl as another high-energy Austrian destination with cross-border terrain into Switzerland.
