65th World Airlines Ski Championships

Mar 22–27, 2026 · Kitzbühel, Austria

● Registration closed

The 65th World Airlines Ski Championships brought the international airline ski community to Kitzbühel for a week combining racing and social events. The published program included competition, lift-ticket options, organized lodging, transfers, welcome events, a themed evening, and a gala dinner.
